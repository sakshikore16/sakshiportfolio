## 1. Monetization Strategy & Revenue Architecture

Revive Threads operates a diversified, multi-stream revenue engine built on four circular monetization pillars:

| Revenue Stream | Monetization Mechanics | Platform Take-Rate / Fee | Blended Gross Margin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. Thrift Marketplace** | P2P & Managed seller commission on sold daily wear | **15% take-rate + ₹49 buyer protection fee** | 78% |
| **02. Occasion Wear Rental** | Managed consignment split on heavy bridal/festive wear | **35% platform share** (65% to garment owner) | 62% (after laundry & logistics) |
| **03. Revive Atelier (Upcycling)** | Platform matchmaking fee on bespoke tailor & design commissions | **20% platform commission** on tailor quotes | 82% |
| **04. NGO & CSR Brand Clearances** | Corporate brand excess stock clearance fees & partner NGO logistics | ₹25/kg handling fee + sponsor brand placement | 55% |
| **05. "Revive Circle" Membership** | Annual subscription: Free delivery, zero deposit on rentals, early drops | **₹999 / year** | 94% |

## 3. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) & CAC Cohort Dynamics

### 3.1 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel
- **Organic Word of Mouth & College Campus Ambassadors**: ₹45 / user.
- **Social Media Viral Loops (Saree Transformation Reels / Thrift Hauls)**: ₹70 / user.
- **Paid Performance Marketing (Meta & Google Shopping)**: ₹180 / user.
- **Blended Year 1 CAC**: **₹110 / transacting customer**.

### 3.2 LTV Calculation (24-Month Horizon)

$$\text{Blended AOV} = (0.65 \times ₹1,250) + (0.25 \times ₹3,800) + (0.10 \times ₹2,600) = ₹812.5 + ₹950 + ₹260 = ₹2,022.50$$

- **Annual Frequency of Transactions**: 3.6 orders / year.
- **Average Customer Lifespan**: 2.5 years.
- **Lifetime Gross Margin**: 31.5% across blended categories.

$$\text{Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)} = 3.6 \times 2.5 \times ₹2,022.50 \times 0.315 = ₹5,733.84$$

$$\text{LTV : CAC Ratio} = \frac{₹5,733.84}{₹110.00} = \mathbf{52.1\times \text{ (Gross LTV)}} \quad \Big| \quad \frac{\text{Net CM2 LTV}}{CAC} = \mathbf{5.8\times}$$

*An LTV/CAC ratio of 5.8x Net indicates exceptional unit economic efficiency and strong venture fundability.*
