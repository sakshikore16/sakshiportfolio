## Slide 2: The Multi-Billion Dollar Crisis in Indian Closets
- **The Paradox**:
  1. **Fast-Fashion Landfill Pressure**: Urban Indian youth buy 18+ garments annually; 60% are discarded within 12 months.
  2. **The "Worn-Once" Festive Debt**: ₹1,20,000+ bridal lehengas and sherwanis are worn for 6 hours, photographed for Instagram, and never repeated.
  3. **The Saree Stash**: 450+ million heritage sarees remain unworn in family trunks because contemporary youth prefer Western or Indo-Western cuts.
  4. **The Instagram Thrift Jungle**: 15,000+ unregulated Instagram sellers—crippled by payment scams, zero returns, unhygienic shipments, and high buyer anxiety.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — The 4-Pillar Circular Ecosystem
- **Unified 4-in-1 Platform**:
  - **01. Thrift (Re-Wear)**: Curated daily apparel (tops, linen shirts, dresses, blazers) at 60–80% off retail with verified measurements & escrow.
  - **02. Rent (Re-Live)**: High-ticket festive & wedding outfits at 10% of retail price with guaranteed steam sanitization & insurance.
  - **03. Revive Atelier (Re-Invent)**: Upload your old saree or worn jeans; choose a modern silhouette; let our vetted master tailors craft your bespoke outfit.
  - **04. Donate (Re-Give)**: Doorstep charity pickup connecting to Goonj & Clothes Box with automated 80G tax exemptions and green reward points.
