## 1. Brand Essence & Narrative

### 1.1 Brand Story
In an era dominated by micro-trends, disposable clothing, and overflowing landfills, Indian wardrobes sit at an ironic crossroads. Millions of urban consumers own closets bursting with barely-worn fast-fashion garments, while wedding and festive apparel costing tens of thousands of rupees are worn once and locked away. Simultaneously, generations of Indian families hold heirloom sarees, vintage textiles, and durable denims rich with memories, yet lacking modern silhouettes.

**Revive Threads** is India's first unified circular fashion ecosystem. We give textiles a second, third, and fourth life by seamlessly integrating **Daily Thrifting**, **Occasion Rental**, **Artisanal Upcycling**, and **Verified NGO Donations** under one tech-enabled platform.

### 1.2 Mission Statement
> *"To eliminate textile waste across urban India by turning every wardrobe into a living, circular asset—making conscious fashion effortless, aspirational, and accessible."*

### 1.3 Vision Statement
> *"To power 10 million circular garment journeys by 2030, establishing India as the global benchmark for heritage-rooted sustainable fashion technology."*

### 1.4 Brand Archetype
- **Primary Archetype: The Conscious Creator (Magician + Artisan)** — Transforming discarded or idle fabrics into bespoke, functional contemporary art.
- **Secondary Archetype: The Mindful Caregiver** — Protecting the earth, honoring traditional artisans, and donating clothing to vulnerable communities with dignity.

## 4. Typography System

### 4.1 Display & Editorial Headline Font: *Cormorant Garamond*
- **Origin**: Designed by Christian Thalmann, inspired by Claude Garamont's 16th-century legacy.
- **Characteristics**: Delicate serifs, ultra-thin hairlines, razor-sharp contrast, aristocratic elegance.
- **Usage**:
  - H1 Hero Titles: 44px – 64px, Line-height: 1.1, Letter-spacing: `-0.01em`
  - H2 Section Titles: 32px – 40px, Line-height: 1.2, Letter-spacing: `0.02em`
  - Italic Accents: Used for editorial subtitles, artisanal quotes, and category badges.

### 4.2 Primary Interface & Body Font: *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Montserrat*
- **Origin**: Modern geometric grotesque with exceptional screen legibility.
- **Characteristics**: Tall x-height, open apertures, geometric clarity, clean numerals for Indian Rupee (`₹`) pricing.
- **Usage**:
  - H3 / Card Titles: Semi-Bold (600), 18px – 22px
  - Body Text: Regular (400) & Medium (500), 14px – 16px, Line-height: 1.6
  - Captions & Microcopy: 11px – 12px, Letter-spacing: `0.05em`
  - Buttons / CTA: Medium (500) & Semi-Bold (600), Uppercase or Title Case with `0.08em` tracking.

### 4.3 Typography Scale Matrix
```
Hero Display       | Cormorant Garamond 56px / Light (300)     | tracking: -0.5px
H1 Section Header  | Cormorant Garamond 38px / Regular (400)   | tracking: 0px
H2 Subheader       | Plus Jakarta Sans 22px / SemiBold (600)  | tracking: -0.2px
Body Large         | Plus Jakarta Sans 16px / Regular (400)   | line-height: 1.6
Body Standard      | Plus Jakarta Sans 14px / Regular (400)   | line-height: 1.5
Label / Pill Tag   | Plus Jakarta Sans 11px / Medium (500)    | tracking: +0.08em (UPPERCASE)
```

## 5. Logo Architecture & Iconography

### 5.1 The Revive Mark (Core Symbol)
- **Geometry**: An infinite thread loop forming a continuous Möbius strip.
- **Motif Details**:
  - The upper arc seamlessly transitions into an organic sprout/leaf, symbolizing botanical renewal.
  - The inner counter-thread mirrors Indian Zari weaving techniques.
  - The central aperture represents the eye of the tailor's needle and the spinning spindle.
- **Line Weight**: Ultra-thin precision stroke (1.5pt – 2.5pt stroke scale) to convey boutique craftsmanship rather than mass industrial manufacturing.

### 5.2 Logo Variations
1. **Primary Horizontal Lockup (`brand/logo_primary.svg`)**:
   - Icon on left, thin divider bar, "REVIVE THREADS" in tracking serif, with category subtitle. Best for Website Navbars, Pitch Deck Headers, and Invoices.
2. **Circular Emblem / App Mark (`brand/logo_icon.svg`)**:
   - Self-contained circular motif. Best for Favicon, Instagram Profile, and App Splash Screens.
3. **Verification Stamp (`brand/logo_badge.svg`)**:
   - Circular seal reading "Revive Threads • Circular Verified • India". Used on physical product tags, sanitization inspection cards, and authenticity seals.

## 7. Packaging & Unboxing Guidelines
1. **100% Plastic-Free**: Certified compostable cassava starch mailers and unbleached cotton dust bags.
2. **Revive Thread Tag**: Recycled seed paper garment tags embedded with basil (tulsi) and marigold seeds that buyers can plant.
3. **Digital Passport QR Code**: Every garment includes a sewn-in or pinned recycled fabric label with a QR code linking to its ownership history, carbon savings, and future resale value.
